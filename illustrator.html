Visual Design

Adobe Creative Cloud

The foundation of my design process. I use Photoshop for photo editing and retouching, Illustrator for logos and vector graphics, InDesign for multi-page layouts like guides and lead magnets, Premiere Pro for video editing, After Effects for 2D motion design and animation, and Firefly for AI-assisted visuals, together, this suite supports nearly every part of my creative work."

MY PROJECTS

Sleepy Owl

A set of hand-drawn logo variations exploring different visual approaches for a brand called Sleepy Owl, from full-body illustrative marks to simplified iconography and custom lettering. Designed to test tone, personality, and versatility across formats.

Business Card

A two-sided business card design built around a warm, playful brand identity, featuring custom branding, QR code integration, and clean contact layout.

Vandelay Industries

Short-form video ad created for Vandelay Industries, optimized for Instagram and Facebook placements. Edited and exported in Adobe Premiere Pro to fit platform-specific formatting and engagement-driven pacing.

Northern Co

A 45–60 second bio video for Northern Co., a Canadian streetwear accessory brand, highlighting their handcrafted rings and accessories. Edited and resized into three formats (landscape, square, vertical) for social media.

Design Dreamz

A short-form animation piece created in After Effects, formatted in vertical (4:5) for Instagram feed. Showcases core motion design and compositing work in a quick, social-ready format."

INFOGRAPHICS

This project required the creation of an infographic illustrating five key ethical and copyright issues relevant to the digital design profession. Using a structured design plan and applying course concepts, I developed a visually engaging piece that combines clear iconography, intentional hierarchy, and thoughtful styling to support comprehension. The final infographic is accompanied by a concise brief explaining my design decisions, ensuring both clarity of message and alignment with professional design standards.

Aether Sigma

Logo design pairing a minimalist cloud-and-sunburst icon with clean typography for a light, modern brand feel.

HERO PAGE

Stardust Expeditions, a luxury interstellar travel company set in the year 2284, approached me to reimagine the hero section of their outdated website. Their goal was to transform a clunky, retro 1990s interface into a cinematic, immersive gateway to deep-space exploration. Using Adobe Illustrator, I created a futuristic, visually striking hero design that captures the wonder of cosmic travel while offering a clearer, more intuitive path to booking voyages across the stars.
