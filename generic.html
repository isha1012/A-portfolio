ABOUT

I’m Fehintola Aishat, a UI/UX and digital designer based in Calgary. I hold a Google UX Design Certification and a B.Sc. in Statistics from the University of Ilorin, which gives me a research-driven approach to design decisions.

I’m currently on a Work-Integrated Learning placement with Bow Valley College’s Web and Digital Team, where I’m helping redesign the Inspire site, contributing to the new ai.bowvalleycollege.ca site, and designing follow-up email campaigns. Earlier, through a dual-client apprenticeship with TB Real Estate Team and Canary Goods Inc., I managed web content on CMS platforms and designed social media and marketing assets in Canva while keeping brand identity consistent across channels. I previously completed a UI/UX design internship at Kiddiebox, where I led research, wireframing, and prototyping from end to end.

Before moving into design, I worked in field coordination with the World Health Organization, managing teams and stakeholders across complex projects, experience that still shapes how I approach collaboration and communication on every design engagement today.
