Tools & Software

Adobe Creative Cloud

The backbone of my design workflow. From editing and retouching photos in Photoshop, to crafting logos and icons in Illustrator, laying out multi-page documents in InDesign, editing video in Premiere Pro, creating 2D motion design and animations in After Effects, to generating AI-assisted visuals in Firefly, this suite covers nearly every stage of my creative process.

Figma

My go-to tool for UI/UX design, wireframing, and prototyping. I use it to design interfaces, map out user flows, and create interactive prototypes before development.

Canva

My go-to tool for quick-turnaround design work, including social media graphics, marketing assets, and Canva-based lead magnets. I use it for creating polished, on-brand visuals efficiently across web and print.

This project required the creation of an infographic illustrating five key ethical and copyright issues relevant to the digital design profession.
